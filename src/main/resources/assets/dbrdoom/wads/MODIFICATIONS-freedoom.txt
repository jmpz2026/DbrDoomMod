This is a MODIFIED, cut-down Freedoom. It is not the original release.

Freedoom Phase 1 (0.13.0) ships 36 maps across four episodes, 28.8MB in all. The
copy bundled with DbrDoomMod is 11.9MB and contains:

  * Episode 1 only, maps E1M1 to E1M9. The other 27 maps were removed.
  * Only the artwork those maps use. 540 of the 963 texture definitions, 578 of
    the 1049 texture patches and 94 flats were removed, along with the music of
    the deleted maps. The TEXTURE1, TEXTURE2 and PNAMES tables were rebuilt to
    match.

Kept regardless of use: every sprite and sound, the switch and animated texture
sets the engine looks up by name, and the sky. Removing any of those breaks the
game rather than shrinking it.

Nothing was altered. Everything still present is bit for bit as Freedoom
released it; the change is purely what was left out, so the mod's download is a
fifth of what it would otherwise be.

The three-clause BSD licence Freedoom uses permits this. The original licence
text and contributor credits are in COPYING-freedoom.txt and
CREDITS-freedoom.txt alongside this file.

For the complete game, with all four episodes and their artwork, get it from the
Freedoom project directly:
    https://freedoom.github.io/

Drop any WAD into this folder and the mod will find it.
